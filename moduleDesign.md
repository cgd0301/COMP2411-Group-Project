# Design

## Subscription:

- Period: seasonly subscription (No automatically subscribing, manual subscribe is compulsory)
- Season to subscribe: Every year has three seasons to subscribe, including spring, summer and autumn.
- Subscription time: if a customer starts subscription, then the subscription length starts from current time to the end of this season. (Price of subscription will adjust according to remian seasonal delivery percentage)
- Subscription window period: customer could start subscribe for the current season immediatly, or book the subscription of next season less than one week before the start of next season.
- If customer sepcifies to start the current subscription. The subscription would take effect immediately since the bill is paid. (e.g. If the everyweek delivery day is Monday, then if customer starts subscribe at Sunday, tommorrow he will get delivered. But if he/she starts on Tuesday, there won' t be any delivery this week, but on next Monday)
- If customer specifies to book future season subscription, the subscription will literally starts at the start date of next season.
- Skipping: refundable for at most two skips (refunding is based on the price of one delivery box according to corresponding plan)

## Plan differences

- Two types of Plan:

1. Farmer's choice (fixed box): the composition of each delivery box is fixed
2. Customizable: the composition of each delivery box could be adjusted, including weight and types of agricultural products (both has a fixed up bound).

- Delivery frequency: weekly/biweekly
- Adjustment (subscription frequency and plan type could not be changed once settled) is not alloed during a full subscription. However, skipping is allowed.

3. Add-on purchases: each plan could enjoy add-on purchase regarding to the products categories included in his/her plan. (e.g. fixed box only has eggs, customizable box could have eggs or milks, then the user subscribing former plan could only add eggs, the latter one could add both two) Add-on purchase costed addtional fee. (Unit price per weight \* add-on weight)
